Floréal Technologies builds open software. Our source is public, our formats
are open, and the data our tools write stays readable without us.

## What we make

[MediaCopy 3000](@/products/mediacopy3000.md) moves camera media between storage
volumes and verifies every copy against an ASC MHL manifest. It is the first
of our tools; the [projects page](@/products/_index.md) lists them all.

## How to reach us

The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Floreal-Technologies), and the
handbooks are at [docs.floreal.tech](https://docs.floreal.tech). Open an issue
in the repository of the tool you are using.
